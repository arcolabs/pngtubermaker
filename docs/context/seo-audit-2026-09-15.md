## OPEN POLICY — blocks the biggest defect (owner: Kai)

**The locale fleet is 90% fake.** 11 non-EN locales × 15 paths are in the
sitemap, but `--locale-probe` found 30/40 sampled variants serve an
**English head** (title/description identical to EN) and, for deeper pages,
identical EN bodies. Roots (`/ja`, `/de`, …) have translated hero bodies but
some contain machine-translation corruption (observed on /ja: "一切都笔画不要").
No page emits hreflang alternates. The root layout's blanket
`canonical:"/"` was removed only for the four pages above — the locale roots
still inherit it because whether to index those URLs AT ALL is Kai's call:

1. **Cut**: drop non-EN locales from sitemap + robots-disallow or 410 the
   routes (one-time cost, honest index).
2. **Translate for real**: head + body per locale + hreflang cluster (large
   effort; the ja corruption needs a human pass regardless).
3. **Half**: keep zh-CN/ja/ko (translated bodies exist on roots), cut the rest.

Scanner's "translated pages ship default-language title/description" MED and
"sitemap/hreflang declare locales whose pages are not translated" HIGH are
BOTH this single decision. Cheapest real improvement is (1) or (3).
